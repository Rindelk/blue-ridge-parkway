# Blue Ridge Parkway & Appalachian Twisties Master Loop
### Hanmer, ON Home Base • 2025 RAM 2500 Cummins Haul • 16-Ft Cargo Trailer • 7-Day Motorcycle Tour • 2,787 Total Miles

Master expedition operational plan, road-snapped navigation package, interactive Web GIS dashboard, and daily saddle timeline Gantt chart for an adventure motorcycle expedition originating from **Hanmer, Ontario**, trailering four Honda motorcycles to **Front Royal, Virginia**, traversing **Skyline Drive**, the entire **Blue Ridge Parkway (MP 0 to MP 469)**, and a legendary Appalachian twisties return loop (**Tail of the Dragon**, **Cherohala Skyway**, and Virginia's **Back of the Dragon**).

---

## 1. Expedition Highlights & Tour Architecture

* **Door-to-Door Expedition Circuit (2,786.6 Miles Total)**:
  - **Stage 0 (Outbound Rig Haul)**: Hanmer, ON $\rightarrow$ Hwy 69 $\rightarrow$ Hwy 400 $\rightarrow$ Queenston-Lewiston Bridge (CBP Entry) $\rightarrow$ US-219 $\rightarrow$ I-99 $\rightarrow$ Front Royal, VA (**720.3 mi / 1,159.2 km**).
  - **Stages 1–7 (Motorcycle Mountain Loop)**: Front Royal Basecamp $\rightarrow$ Skyline Drive (105 mi) $\rightarrow$ Blue Ridge Parkway (469 mi) $\rightarrow$ Tail of the Dragon (US-129) $\rightarrow$ Cherohala Skyway $\rightarrow$ Back of the Dragon (VA-16) $\rightarrow$ Front Royal Basecamp (**1,342.8 mi / 2,160.7 km**).
  - **Stage 8 (Return Rig Haul)**: Front Royal, VA $\rightarrow$ I-81 $\rightarrow$ I-99 $\rightarrow$ US-219 $\rightarrow$ Queenston-Lewiston Bridge (CBSA Entry) $\rightarrow$ Hwy 400 $\rightarrow$ Hwy 69 $\rightarrow$ Hanmer, ON (**723.5 mi / 1,164.3 km**).
* **Tow Rig & Trailer Basecamp**: 2025 RAM 2500 Heavy Duty Crew Cab Cummins 6.7L Turbo Diesel towing a 16-foot tandem-axle enclosed cargo trailer (~40 ft total combined rig). Staged and secured at **Eagle's Nest RV, Boat & Vehicle Storage** (3434 Guard Hill Rd, Front Royal, VA), just 4.5 miles from Skyline Drive's northern entrance.
* **Crown Jewel Mountain Summits & Civil Engineering Wonders**:
  - **Mount Mitchell Summit Spur (MP 355.4)**: Highest point in mainland North America east of the Mississippi River (6,684 ft / 2,037 m).
  - **Linn Cove Viaduct (MP 304.4)**: S-curve cantilever bridge hugging the rugged face of Grandfather Mountain.
  - **Mabry Mill (MP 176.1)**: Historic working gristmill, millpond, and buckwheat pancake breakfast.
  - **James River Footbridge (MP 63.7)**: Lowest elevation on the Blue Ridge Parkway (649 ft).
  - **Richland Balsam Overlook (MP 431.4)**: Highest motor road point on the Parkway (6,053 ft / 1,845 m).
  - **27 Mountain Tunnels**: Mary's Rock Tunnel (Skyline MP 32.2) plus all 26 Blue Ridge Parkway rock tunnels.
* **The Legendary Appalachian Twisties**:
  - **Tail of the Dragon (US-129)**: 318 banked curves in 11 miles of unbroken asphalt switchbacks.
  - **Cherohala Skyway (NC-143 / TN-165)**: Mile-high sweeping ridge curves rising to 5,390 ft across the Unicoi Crest.
  - **Back of the Dragon (VA Route 16)**: 32 miles and 300+ hairpins climbing over three mountain ranges between Marion and Tazewell, VA.

## 5. GPS Device Import Instructions

### For Garmin Zūmo XT / XT2 & Montana:
1. Connect device to your computer via USB (or insert microSD card).
2. Copy [**blue_ridge_parkway_loop.gpx**](file:///C:/Users/Jamie/Desktop/Blue%20Ridge%20Parkway/blue_ridge_parkway_loop.gpx) into the `Garmin/GPX/` folder on the device.
3. Disconnect device $\rightarrow$ open **Apps $\rightarrow$ Tracks** $\rightarrow$ select tracks and tap **Convert to Trip**.

### For Garmin BaseCamp (PC/Mac):
1. Open BaseCamp $\rightarrow$ File $\rightarrow$ **Import into 'My Collection'**.
2. Select [**blue_ridge_parkway_loop.gpx**](file:///C:/Users/Jamie/Desktop/Blue%20Ridge%20Parkway/blue_ridge_parkway_loop.gpx).
3. The 9 stages will appear as distinct color-coded tracks with all 135 waypoints placed precisely at highway exits, truck stops, and mountain overlooks.

### For Gaia GPS / OsmAnd / Scenic:
1. Open Gaia GPS web portal or mobile app $\rightarrow$ **Import GPX/KML** $\rightarrow$ select [**blue_ridge_parkway_loop.gpx**](file:///C:/Users/Jamie/Desktop/Blue%20Ridge%20Parkway/blue_ridge_parkway_loop.gpx).
2. Enable offline map downloads for Ontario, New York, Pennsylvania, Maryland, Virginia, North Carolina, and Tennessee.
